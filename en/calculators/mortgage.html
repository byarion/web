How the payment is calculated

This calculator uses the most common system, constant payments (the French method): you pay the same amount each month, and at first most of it is interest, which falls over time.

Payment = principal × i ÷ (1 − (1 + i)−n)

Where i is the monthly interest rate (the annual rate divided by 12) and n is the number of months.

Fixed or variable mortgage

If your mortgage is fixed, enter its rate as it is. If it is variable, the rate is the Euribor plus a spread, so you can add the two for an estimate. That payment will change when the rate is reviewed.

What it does not include

The result is indicative. It does not include fees, insurance, valuation or notary costs, or taxes. For the exact payment, check your bank's offer.

How to use the calculator step by step

  1. Enter the amount you are going to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate. On a variable loan, add the Euribor and the spread.
  3. Choose the term in years.
  4. You will see the monthly payment. Try several terms and rates to compare.

What changes if the interest rate changes

With €150,000 over 25 years: at 2%, €635.78; at 3%, €711.32; at 4%, €791.76; at 5%, €876.89. Each percentage point adds roughly €75 to €85 a month. That is, broadly, what would happen on a variable mortgage if the Euribor rises. See the guide on fixed, variable or mixed mortgages.

Common mistakes

  • Looking only at the payment and forgetting the total interest and the term.
  • Not counting insurance, fees, taxes and purchase costs, which are not in this payment.
  • Not leaving a margin in case rates rise on a variable loan.

Frequently asked questions

What would the payment be on a €150,000 mortgage over 25 years?

At 3% interest, about €711 a month. Change the figures above to see your case.

Does it work for a personal or car loan too?

Yes. Enter the loan amount, the annual interest and the term.

What payment would I make on a €200,000 mortgage over 25 years?

At 3% interest, about €948 a month. At 4% it would be about €1,056, so the difference between rates matters.

How much interest do I pay in total on €150,000 over 25 years?

At 3%, about €63,400 in interest over the whole loan, not counting fees or insurance.

Is a long or a short term better?

A short term raises the payment but reduces total interest. A long one makes the payment more comfortable, but you pay more interest. It depends on how much monthly margin you have.
