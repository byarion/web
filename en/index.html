What is inflation?

The general rise in prices. At 3%, the same money buys you 3% less than a year ago.

Compound interest

The interest you earn also earns interest. That's why starting early, even small, makes a big difference.

What is the Ibex 35?

Spain's main stock index, made up of the 35 largest companies listed on the Spanish market.

Emergency fund

A cushion of 3 to 6 months of expenses set aside for surprises, before thinking about investing.
