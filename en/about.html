About Ondexi

Ondexi is an independent editorial project that explains economics and personal finance in plain language, so anyone can understand the money they deal with every day.

What you will find here

  • Guides to learn concepts such as inflation, the Euribor, income tax in Spain, index funds or how to choose a mortgage.
  • Free calculators (VAT, mortgage, inflation, compound interest, net salary) with the formula explained and worked examples.
  • Markets with indicative data on how the main indices are moving.

Who we are

We are the Ondexi team. We write, review and update all the content on the site. We are not a financial institution or an asset manager, and we do not sell investment products: our aim is to explain, not to recommend.

How we work

  • We write each guide in clear language, starting from the basics and using examples with numbers.
  • We check data against official and public sources, such as the European Central Bank, Spain's national statistics office (INE), the tax agency or the Bank of Spain.
  • We show the formulas behind our calculators so you can check the result.
  • We review and update pages when data or regulations change.

Where the data comes from

  • Euribor: data published by the European Central Bank.
  • Markets: quotes from public data providers, which may be delayed.

What we do not do

Ondexi is for information and education only. Nothing we publish is personalised financial, tax or legal advice or a recommendation to buy or sell. Calculator results are indicative. Before making an important decision, consult a professional or your bank.

Advertising

The site may show Google AdSense ads, which help us keep the project running. Advertising does not influence what we write or how we write it.

Errors and suggestions

If you spot a mistake or want to suggest a topic, write to us from the contact page. We correct errors as soon as we find them.
